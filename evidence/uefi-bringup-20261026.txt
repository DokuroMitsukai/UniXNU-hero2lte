Procedure
---------

The image was pushed to /tmp in TWRP, written to /dev/block/sda5 with dd,
synced, and the partition was read back with sha256sum.  The readback hash
matched the image before each boot.

Results
-------

1. safemem: reached the Project Silicium UEFI UI and the expected
   "failed to find an operating system" screen.

2. bringup: the 120 px block at the top left alternated magenta and cyan about
   once per second.

3. bringup: key presses produced the expected bars:
   Volume Up red, Volume Down green, Home blue, Power white.

4. bringup: white counter blocks appeared at the lower left and advanced with
   accepted key events.

5. The phone was manually rebooted to TWRP after the test.  It did not reset by
   itself while the probe was running.

Not covered
-----------

XNU handoff, ArmCpu GICv2 support, ARM generic-timer events through the XNU
interrupt path, storage, and power management were not exercised.  BringupTestDxe
is diagnostic and is not part of the XNU firmware configuration.
